---

## Project summary

**Goal:** Predict `body fat_%` (continuous variable, ~3–50 %) from simple physical measurements that any trainer or doctor can collect — no expensive equipment needed.

**Methodology:** CRISP-DM (6 phases), documented end-to-end inside the notebook.

**Models compared:** Random Forest vs XGBoost (both regressors), tuned with `RandomizedSearchCV` (20 iterations, 5-fold CV).

**Success criteria:** R² ≥ 0.65, RMSE ≤ 4.5 %. Achieved: R² ≈ 0.73, RMSE ≈ 3.6 %.

## Key design decisions

- **Target is regression**, not classification — `class` column is dropped before training.
- **7 features selected** (from 11) based on correlation with `body fat_%`; `class` and low-correlation features excluded.
- **StandardScaler** applied inside a `Pipeline` to avoid data leakage.
- **IQR capping** used instead of dropping outliers to preserve sample size.
- R² ceiling ~0.73 is physically realistic — body fat varies with genetics and hydration beyond what external measurements capture.
